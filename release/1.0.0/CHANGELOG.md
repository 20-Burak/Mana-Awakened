# 1.0.0 - first public release

Mana Awakened is a manhwa-style magic cultivation mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

- **Ten Circles of cultivation**: meditate to fill and train your mana core, break through from Mortal to Transcendent Deity, survive the backlash of a failed breakthrough and the heavenly trials of the higher Circles.
- **Meditation** in a lotus pose on solid ground, broken by moving, damage or being pushed; **Meditation Circles** of Rune Plates (five tiers), leylines, mana-rich biomes and the **Mana Condenser** (Forge Energy) for faster training.
- **Purity, body refinements and the Primordial Dragon Heart**: absorb rare materials and the dragon's heart to temper your body and open the 7th Circle and beyond.
- **The Inscription Altar**: 105 sigils unlocked by logic puzzles, 71 fighting sigils in seventeen kinds and ten elements, 34 working spells (find ores and structures, mine veins, fell trees, bridge gaps, travel home or to other worlds, change the weather and the time), and fifteen modifiers to shape your own spells.
- **Elements that change the world**: fire melts sand into glass, frost freezes lava into obsidian, storms bring real thunderstorms; everything borrowed is given back, and servers can switch it off.
- **Every spell its own show**: magic circles, condensing spells, meteors falling on slanted paths, volleys that open all their circles before they launch, and a body gesture for every kind of spell.
- **Fifty passive skills**, five per Circle, learned from crafted scrolls.
- **Shared mana** with Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Ars Nouveau and Mahou Tsukai: one pool, their costs and damage scaled with your Circle.
- **Version-tolerant integration**: tested with the oldest and newest builds of each (Iron's 1.21-3.4.4 to 1.21.1-3.16.3, Ars Nouveau 5.8.2 to 5.13.3, Mahou Tsukai 1.21.0-v1.35.12 to 1.21.1-v1.36.28); newer builds are always accepted, and a part the installed build lacks is switched off on its own with one warning instead of stopping the game. Each mod can be taken out of the shared pool in the server config.
- **The Cultivator's Codex** explains everything in game; English and Turkish.
