# Mana Awakened

**Manhwa-style magic cultivation for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).** Sit in meditation, gather the mana of the world into your core and break through ten Circles, from Mortal to Transcendent Deity. Write your own spells at the Inscription Altar and watch every cast unfold: magic circles open, the spell takes shape, and meteors, novas and volleys land where you aimed.

Requires **Player Animator** (any 2.x build for 1.21.1) on the client and the server.

## Cultivation: the Circles

You carry a **mana core**. Meditate (**K**) on solid ground: you sink into a lotus pose, absorb mana four times as fast, and once the core is full it **trains** and grows. When it reaches the threshold of your Circle, hold **B** to break through.

Your thresholds are your own: **your next threshold is five times the core you break through with, and your cap is twice your threshold.** Break through at the cap and every later number grows ten times per Circle instead of five, up to 500 billion at Circle 9; every Circle you leave full also doubles your training speed, so the road is just as long, only grander.

- **Ten Circles**, each with a bigger core, stronger spells, new sigils and passives. At Circle 10 mana is infinite and every spell, from every mod, is free.
- **Breakthroughs can fail**: a backlash empties and fractures your core; a mortal body cannot hold the 7th Circle at all.
- **Heavenly trials** from the 5th Circle on: the heavens send guardians, and you ascend only if you defeat them all.
- **Meditation Formations** of Rune Plates (five tiers, up to ×32 training), **leylines** and mana-rich biomes, and the **Mana Condenser**, which turns Forge Energy into thick mana. Crops grow faster in it too.
- **Purity** (0 to 150), **body refinements** from rare materials (Warden Meridians, Nether Star, Netherite, Echo Shard...) and the **Primordial Dragon Heart** of the Ender Dragon temper your body.

## Spells: the Inscription Altar

A sigil is understood, not bought: solve its ring of glyphs, a small logic puzzle with exactly one answer, and it is yours.

- **105 sigils**: 71 fighting sigils in seventeen kinds (beams, strikes, meteors, novas, pillars, cones, waves, barrages, rains, vortices, wards, heals, sanctuaries, buffs, curses, dashes, blinks) and ten elements, no two alike.
- **34 working spells**: find ores (up to ten at once, each glowing in its own colour) and structures of any mod, mine a whole vein, fell a tree, build bridges and ramparts, travel home, to your anchors or to other worlds, change the weather and the time, rewind a crater.
- **Fifteen modifiers** (Power, Range, Multi-cast, Radius, Echo, Chain, Pierce, Leech...) shape each spell; create as many as your book holds.
- **The elements change the world**: fire fuses sand into glass, frost freezes lava into obsidian, storms bring real thunderstorms. What spells borrow is given back, and servers can switch all of it off.
- **Every cast is a show**: a magic circle opens, the spell condenses, travels and hits on its own clock, and your character casts it with a gesture of its own. Volleys open all their circles before they launch.

## Passive skills

Fifty passives, five per Circle, learned from crafted scrolls: flight, a mana barrier, a full stomach from mana, a second wind and more. One per Circle is active.

## Shared mana with other magic mods

With **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks**, **Ars Nouveau** or **Mahou Tsukai**, their mana is your core: one pool, refilled by meditation. Their costs grow gently with your core (a 10-mana spell costs about 34 at a core of 5,000), their damage with your Circle, and at Circle 10 they are free. Tested with Iron's Spells 1.21-3.4.4 to 1.21.1-3.16.3, Ars Nouveau 5.8.2 to 5.13.3 and Mahou Tsukai 1.21.0-v1.35.12 to 1.21.1-v1.36.28; newer builds are accepted. Each mod can be left out of the shared pool in the server config.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| K | Meditate |
| B (hold) | Breakthrough |
| N (hold) | Spell wheel |
| U | Cast |
| ] / [ | Next / previous spell |
| O | Passive skills |
| J | Quick target menu of a working spell |

All keys can be rebound; the defaults avoid the keys of vanilla, Iron's Spells, Ars Nouveau, Mahou Tsukai and Curios.

## Configuration

The HUD (style, the calm or classic look of the Circle-10 heart, corner, offset, size), the amount of spell effects drawn in the world, and on the server every balance number, the terrain switches (environment, destruction, sky, teleport) and the trials. Every new player gets a **Cultivator's Codex** that explains the whole mod in game.
