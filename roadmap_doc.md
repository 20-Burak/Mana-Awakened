## 1.0.2, the external-cost hotfix

From the owner's 1.0.1 play-test: at Circle 4 with a pool of 2.15M and 1,759 mana, a Mahou Tsukai spell whose cost on the HUD was
232,321 was cast anyway and the popup said "-1,056"; with a full core the same spell paid "-289,336".

- **The cause:** Mahou Tsukai's `PlayerManaManager.drainMana(player, cost, ...)` checks the cost against its own stored mana and takes
  all of it or nothing (scrolls, staffs, circles and the per-tick drains of channelled spells compare the result with the cost and do
  nothing when it falls short). The adapter showed Mahou the core unscaled (`floor(core)`), so a 483-mana spell passed Mahou's check
  at a core of 1,759; the adapter then charged 483 x 481 and `ManaData.spendMana` clamped at 0, taking what the core had. Iron's
  Spells had the same hole for channelled spells (its 10-tick check, `mana - 2 x cost >= 0`, ran against the unscaled core, and
  `castSpell` charges `max(0, mana - cost)` without a check); Ars Nouveau already checked the scaled cost
  (`SpellCostCalcEvent.Pre`) but asked for the cost rounded up, refusing a core that held a fractional cost exactly.
- **The rule (all three mods, server side):** an external spell with scaled cost C starts only if the core holds C
  (`ExternalMagic.affords`, a rounding allowance of a millionth). The shared spend (`MagicAdapters.reportSpent`) is all or nothing:
  the whole C or, when the core does not hold it, nothing (no mana, no heat, no popup); never a part.
- **What each mod is shown:** Mahou always, and Iron's from the moment a cast is allowed until it ends, see the whole units of their
  own mana the core can pay at the multiplier (`MagicAdapters.mirrorUnits`, `ExternalMagic.affordableUnits`): a core of 1,759 at
  x481 shows Mahou 3. Their own checks, made in their own units, then agree exactly with the core, and whatever they take the core
  pays in full. Between casts Iron's sees the core as a whole number, so its own first check lets a cast through to
  `SpellPreCastEvent`, which refuses with the scaled cost. Ars keeps the core as its mana and is asked the scaled cost rounded up,
  or no more than the core holds when the core holds it (`ArsNouveauAdapter.checkedCost`); it is shown at most 2,147,483,646 so a
  cost it cannot pay is refused at any size. Mahou's maximum stays the capacity (Gandr's cost and damage scale with it).
- **Mahou's own regeneration** (every 100 ticks in its `PlayerTickEvent.Post` handler, possibly after ours) is overwritten by a
  lowest-priority listener after every player tick, so it never reaches a cast.
- **Feedback:** "Not enough mana in your core: this spell costs N" (the existing `mana_awakened.integration.not_enough`) for a
  refused Iron's cast (as before), Ars spell (new, besides Ars's own chat line) and Mahou scroll (new: `LivingEntityUseItemEvent.Stop`
  of a scroll whose cost is known, plain scroll casts and Gandr).
- **Unchanged:** the cost formula, `externalCostScale`, the Circle-10 rule, external spell power, Mahou's growth bookkeeping, our own
  sigils; no payload changed (protocol 24).
- **Verification:** 353 game tests PLAIN and WITH Iron's / Ars / Mahou ("ours" build set), 9 new (`ExternalGateGameTests`); a mutation
  for every new rule (`verify-tools/mutate-102.ps1`).

## 1.0.1, the progression retune

From the owner's 1.0.0 play-test and his model sheet (`mana_taban_hiz_v4.xlsx`); the numbers are his.

- **Personal thresholds:** Circle 0 keeps 500. From then on the threshold is **five times the core the player broke through with**
  (`core.thresholdFactor`, replacing the table factor `thresholdGrowth`) and the **cap is twice the threshold** for everyone (Q and
  `ceilingFloor` are gone). A breakthrough still doubles the core, so every Circle starts at 40% of its own threshold. Always leaving
  at the cap gives 500, 5,000, 50,000 ... 500 billion at Circle 9; always at the threshold the old table, 500, 2,500 ... 976.6 million
  (the reference threshold E_ref). The threshold is stored per player (`ManaData` format 8, `threshold`) and synced.
- **Full-training bonus:** b = 2 (`core.fullTrainingFactor`, replacing `fullTrainingBonus` = 1.5 so existing config files get the new
  value), and Circle 0's fullness now counts too: Bt = product of (1 + s) over every Circle left. Bt equals the own threshold over
  E_ref, so the hours to the threshold are the same on every path (1.11 h in Circle 0, 4.17 in Circle 1 ... 6,358 in Circle 9).
- **Saves of 1.0.0:** threshold = E_ref(circle) x product of (1 + s) of the stored history; Circle, core, purity, refinements and
  history kept; core and mana clamped to the new cap. The floor of a core is the start core of the shortest route into its Circle,
  so no core is raised past what it had. The owner's own Circle-1 save (core 4,984, Circle 0 left at its cap) gets 5,000 / 10,000:
  not ready any more (16 short), x2 training.
- **Numbers beyond an int:** every amount is a double or a long; the spend packet carries a double (it was a float). Audited, no
  change needed: Iron's Spells keeps its mana in a float, but its `max_mana` attribute stops at 1,000,000 and Iron's clamps its mana
  to it, so it sees at most a million of the core and every spend inside that is read exactly (checked at a core of a trillion). Ars
  Nouveau's maximum and Mahou Tsukai's mana are ints: they see at most 2,147,483,647.
- **Compact numbers:** from 1,000,000 on, three significant digits cut (never rounded up) and a unit from the language file (1.23M,
  45.6M, 4.56B, 7.89T; Turkish Mn, Mr, Tn) on the HUD's mana and core lines, the training tooltip, spend popups, the drain readout,
  the selected-spell cost, the Spell Wheel, the Altar's cost lines and Iron's "not enough mana" line (`core/CompactNumber`,
  `client/hud/HudNumbers`). `/mana` keeps full numbers and gains a threshold line.
- **The Meditation Formation:** the Rune-Plate structure is a "Meditation Formation" (Turkish "Meditasyon Dizilimi") in every text, and
  the HUD says "Formation tier 5" ("Dizilim kademesi 5"); the cultivation Circle keeps its name. Block ids, registry names and config
  keys unchanged.
- **Other mods' spells ten times cheaper:** cost = base x max(1, `integration.externalCostScale` x (capacity / 100)^alpha), scale 0.1
  (1.0 = the scaling of 1.0.0). A 10-mana Firebolt costs 10 at 1,000, about 34 at 5,000, about 144 at 25,000.
- **Commands:** `/mana set circle <players> <circle> [fullness]` puts a player on a path (`... 9 1` = always at the cap).
- **Verification:** 344 game tests PLAIN and WITH Iron's / Ars / Mahou ("ours" build set); a mutation for every new rule caught.

## 2. Decisions

Settled in v0.2.0:
- **Keys:** M and B stay the defaults; players rebind in Controls if a pack conflicts (Mahou Tsukai also uses M).
- **Mana ownership:** Mana Awakened owns the pool. Murim's mana mode should be `OFF` when both are installed (Murim can later detect `mana_awakened` and switch itself off).
- **Ancient Dragon God:** a second heart at Circle 9+; +40 HP total, +10% breakthrough chance.
- **Drops:** one heart per kill, drop chance configurable; extra bosses go in the `dragon_heart_bearers` entity tag (Hydra, Ignis, Ender Golem pre-listed as optional).

Still open: nothing blocking. Both earlier questions were settled in v0.4.0 (built-in spell set; wheel on R, cast on G, rebindable).

## 4. Rules for every phase

- **Save format:** bump `ManaData.DATA_VERSION`, migrate old values in `fromStorage`, and add a game test that loads old data.
- **Network:** bump `ModNetwork.PROTOCOL_VERSION` whenever a payload changes, so mismatched versions are refused at login instead of misbehaving.
- **Tests:** every phase adds game tests next to the existing ones (`.\gradlew.bat runGameTestServer`), and every new behaviour is checked by deliberately breaking it once to see a test fail.
- **Release checklist:**
  1. `build`
  2. `runGameTestServer`
  3. A `runClient` smoke test
  4. `deployToInstances`
  5. Launch SakinPack and AS once each.
